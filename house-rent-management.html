Personal Project

House Rent Management System

A complete offline desktop application for managing flats, tenants, monthly rent generation, payment records, receipts, repairs, reports, backups, audit logs, and tenant move-out settlements.

JavaFX SQLite Maven Apache PDFBox Apache POI jpackage Installer

Core Modules

Tenant & Flat Management

Manage active tenants, past tenants, flat occupancy, meter numbers, move-in details, move-out lifecycle, and optional security deposits.

Rent & Receipt Workflow

Generate monthly rent, track due/partial/late/paid status, archive paid records, and print PDF receipts with consistent receipt numbers.

Reports & Accounting Logic

Separate rent income, discounts, utilities, security deposits, and owner-paid repairs for cleaner income, due, repair, and net-profit reporting.

Repair Tracking

Track repair category, cost, paid-by status, completion status, vendor name, vendor phone, invoice number, and repair notes.

Security & Recovery

Includes login, optional save login, logout cleanup, password change, recovery PIN, emergency recovery keys, backup, restore, and factory reset.

Audit Logs

Important actions such as login, logout, payment, backup, restore, reset, tenant changes, repair updates, and receipt actions are recorded.
