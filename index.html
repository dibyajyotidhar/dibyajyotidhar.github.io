About Me

I'm a Software Quality Assurance Engineer who believes that quality isn't just a phase — it's a mindset embedded in every step of the development lifecycle.

Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from North South University. I began my career in IT support, building a strong understanding of systems and user workflows before transitioning into QA engineering — a path that gave me a uniquely holistic view of how software breaks and why it matters.

Today I design and execute test strategies across manual and automation domains. I've worked on real-world government and commercial platforms, and I'm actively growing my skills in test automation with Selenium, Playwright, and Cypress. I'm passionate about making software more reliable, one test case at a time.
